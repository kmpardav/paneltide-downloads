# PanelTide 0.11.0 Windows preview — getting started

Download the Windows x64 installer or portable EXE for 0.11.0 and check its SHA-256 against the release checksums. These are unsigned previews, so Windows may report an unrecognized publisher; decide for yourself before running them. No security setting needs to change. The local companion is bundled, so no Node or terminal is needed. Your Photoshop or Clip Studio licences and any AI-provider access are separate.

Close PanelTide before installing. Installing over 0.10 or 0.9 keeps your work, because saved data lives in `%APPDATA%\PanelTide\workspace` and not in the installation folder. Make a backup in **Backup and recovery** first if you have work you cannot recreate. If you ever started PanelTide from inside another app installed from the Microsoft Store and your comics seem missing, see Troubleshooting in the guide.

1. Open **Your comics** and choose **Create a new comic**, or open an existing one. For a long comic or issue, use **All places → Large collections** instead.
2. Export static PNG pages from your drawing app and add them. PanelTide keeps the exact original files; the smaller review previews are separate and are not print masters.
3. In the **Review workspace**, add your intent and set the **Page layout** (printed facing pages, vertical scroll or one page at a time). In **Page studio**, you can mark the panels of imported pages. In **Script and story bible**, import a script, map beats to pages and approve the rules you want reviews to respect.
4. In **AI connections**, check a supported route: a signed-in Codex with **Check connection with a sample page**, or an optional API or local model. Only the shown material is sent, and only when you explicitly analyze.
5. Plan a review in **Comic review** (or **Large-work review** for a collection), prepare it, inspect exactly what will be shared, and analyze. Coverage shows what was and was not assessed.
6. Open received advice as manual work: keep choices intentional, make tasks, copy a tool brief, or open **Prepare an optional artwork edit**. Compare saved replies in **Managing editor**.
7. Revise pages in your drawing app and use **Bring updated pages back** to replace or add pages explicitly, then review changed artwork again.
8. Use **Backup and recovery** for complete work backups. Unsaved inputs in most windows are kept on this computer and can be restored explicitly after a restart.

Every page has the same header. **All places** lists every area, and the **User guide** link opens the Creator's guide, which explains every area with its current limits. Report problems at https://github.com/kmpardav/paneltide-downloads/issues/new/choose with the version, Windows version, steps and visible error. Remove private artwork, credentials and account details first.
